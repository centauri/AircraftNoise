Method

Each pass contributes its loudest 10 s LAeq and the straight-line distance to the microphone at that moment. The level is corrected to a reference distance of 1,000 m with spherical spreading, 20·log10(d / 1000), plus air absorption of 1 dB per km. Arrivals and departures are ranked separately because thrust differs; the combined view mixes both. Medians and the 25–75% range are shown, and a type needs the chosen minimum of passes to appear. Type names are folded into families, so "Boeing 737NG 8K2/W", "B738" and "737-8K2" count as one 737-800. The sensor measures what it hears at this address; another address hears another ranking. Aircraft identities come from the Mictronics aircraft database via tar1090-db; airline names from Wikidata.
