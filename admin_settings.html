Shown on the public page. The first heading is the station name: it appears in the page title, quietly in the header, and on the map card. Later sections stay in About this station. The page stays unchanged when this list is empty. Markup is not accepted.

Community monitors

Share this station with the worldwide database at data.aircraftnoise.eu and on every install's community page. The shared point is the public decoy position, moved by the privacy radius (200 m by default). The sensor id, the raw noise samples and the exact coordinates stay on this server. Sharing is on from the first start: that is what a station is for. Untick it here to keep this station to yourself; nothing more is sent, and what was shared stays under the station's id without any link to you. A new station is recorded at once but joins the public map and totals only after a week of steady reports, or straight away when you claim it below.

Every ten minutes the in-zone passes since the last confirmed send are sent, with two hours of overlap, so an outage of up to a week loses nothing: time, aircraft type, operator, runway and route, peak and LAmax, and the distance to the aircraft. Never the aircraft position or the sensor id. This is what makes the worldwide statistics possible.
The address visitors should open. A LAN address is listed as local only.
Receives the record and the passes and serves the station list. The default is data.aircraftnoise.eu; only change it for a private network.

Every in-zone pass this station ever recorded goes to the data service, a week at a time, in the background. Safe to repeat: the service keeps one copy of each pass. A changed address keeps the station id, so nothing is lost or doubled.
The install makes its own key on the first share. Paste a new one here only when you rotated it on your account page at the data service. 

                

                    
                    
                

            

            
Claim this station

            
An account at data.aircraftnoise.eu lets you rotate the key, unlink the station, search every stored pass, or ask for the station's data to be removed. Claiming also puts a new station on the public map at once, without waiting the week. Make a claim code there and enter it here; the install sends it with its station key.

Scraper guard

            
Every address gets a budget of pages and JSON calls per minute and hour; past it the answer is a 429 with Retry-After. An address that keeps going, follows the hidden trap link, reads pages with a script, or keeps collecting refused answers is blocked for 15 minutes, then an hour, 6 hours, a day, a week. Verified search engines get ten times the page budget. The admin pages, logins, the setup, this machine, the addresses below and you while signed in here are never judged.
